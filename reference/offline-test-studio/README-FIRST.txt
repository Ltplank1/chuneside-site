=============================================

- Show current view, Like and follower achievements directly on artist profiles.
- Keep referral points marked for the future hosted edition, where real sign-up attribution can be verified.
- Edit every hero text field: small label, both headline lines, description and button wording.
- Upload a replacement logo, choose its size and position, or restore the supplied ChuneSide logo.
- See live song totals beneath every region and genre, including zero-count categories.
- Sort discovery results by recently added, oldest, most viewed, most liked or A–Z.
- Count a local view whenever a track or music video is played in this offline test edition.
- Filter the monthly and all-time verified Like charts by genre.
- Change the homepage hero background to your own image or looping video.
- Filter every region by Soca, Calypso, Reggae, Dancehall, Hip-Hop, Rap, R&B, Gospel or Other.
- Choose genres from a dropdown when adding releases.
- Selecting Other opens a field for a custom genre.
- Maintain an artist database and reuse artist names from a dropdown.
- Click an artist to open their complete ChuneSide catalogue.
- Edit artist names, territories and existing release details.
- Demonstrate email/password registration, sign-in and member-only Likes.
- Demonstrate the Google quick-sign-up journey with a clearly marked offline button.
- Apply Carnival, Ocean Night, Christmas or custom colour themes from Test Studio.
- Review new members and their email addresses from Manage members.
- Edit, freeze, block, unblock or delete a member account.
- Add artist pictures, biographies, genres and social-media links.
- Block an artist to hide their profile and releases without deleting the catalogue.
- Use one hero image, one looping video, or a slideshow of up to six images.
- Apply glitch, fade, slide or static presentation to hero media.
- Turn a scrolling or fixed announcement banner on or off.
- Edit the public How to Submit instructions and submission email addresses.
- Control announcement scrolling from 15 to 90 seconds.

- Disable visitor theme choices whenever the Studio theme must be enforced.
- Build a hero sequence by adding images and videos one at a time.
- Keep up to 10 mixed image and video hero items.
- Move any individual hero item up or down without replacing the others.
- Remove one hero item while leaving the rest of the sequence untouched.

INDIVIDUAL HERO MEDIA LIBRARY

1. Open Test Studio > Hero media library.
2. Choose one image or one video and click Add selected item & save.
3. The manager remains open so you can choose the next individual item.
4. Use the up and down arrows to rearrange only the selected item.
5. Use Remove to delete only that image or video.
6. Choose an effect and the time each item stays on screen.
7. The supplied ChuneSide hero returns automatically when the custom library is empty.

MESSAGE DISPLAY CONTROLS

- Open Test Studio > Message display.
- Add up to six messages. Each message has an independent Seconds field, including 60 seconds for a full minute.
- Continuous Scroll uses the separate Scroll speed control; Slow is 40 seconds and Extra Slow is 90 seconds.
- Fade, Drop and Rise rotate through the messages using each message's own display time.
- Fixed Messages rotate cleanly without a movement effect.
- Letter styles include Clean, Heavy Bold, LED Display and Fun Rounded.

VISITOR THEME CHOICES

- Open Test Studio > Seasonal themes and enable Let visitors choose their own theme.
- Visitors then see Appearance at the top of the site and can select a look for their own device.
- Turn the option off to hide Appearance and enforce the Studio colour scheme.

MEMBER PRIVACY AND MODERATION

- The Test Studio displays member names, email addresses, signup type and account status.
- Passwords are protected and are never displayed in the management area.
- Frozen members may sign in and listen, but cannot Like or Follow.
- Blocked members cannot sign in. Their existing ranking activity remains stored unless the member is deleted.
- Deleting a member also removes that member's Likes and Follows.

ARTIST PROFILES AND MODERATION

- Add a portrait, biography, primary genre, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube and website links.
- Click an artist's name on a release to open the artist profile and full ChuneSide catalogue.
- Blocking an artist hides their profile, releases, videos and chart entries from public sections.
- Unblocking restores the artist and catalogue without requiring another upload.

DYNAMIC HERO AND ANNOUNCEMENT BANNER

- Choose one image, one MP4/WebM video, or 2–6 slideshow images.
- Choose Glitch pulse, Soft fade, Cinematic slide or No animation.
- Set a slideshow speed of 4, 6, 8 or 10 seconds.
- The scrolling banner can be disabled, positioned at the top or bottom, and set to scrolling or fixed.

HOW TO SUBMIT

- The public guide starts with MP3 audio, MP4 video, 3000 × 3000 pixel square artwork and a clear high-resolution artist portrait.
- Open Test Studio > Submission guide to change any requirement or add one or more submission email addresses.

ARTIST CATALOGUES AND EDITING

1. Open Test Studio and find Artist database.
2. Click an artist name to see every release uploaded under that artist.
3. Use Edit artist to correct the name or home territory. Name changes are also applied to that artist's releases.
4. Under Your releases, click Edit to change a title, artist, genre, origin or creation type.
5. You can leave the media and artwork fields blank while editing to keep the existing files.

TEST MEMBER SIGN-UP

1. Click Sign up at the top of the site.
2. Create a local demonstration account with a display name, email and password.
3. Use the Sign in tab to test returning with the same details.
4. The Continue with Google button demonstrates the quick-sign-up journey using the name and email entered in the form.

Google does not verify a real account in this offline edition. Real Google sign-in requires a secure OAuth connection and will be added to the live website.

CHANGE THE SITE COLOURS

1. Open Test Studio and choose Seasonal themes.
2. Select a prepared theme, including Carnival's pink, orange and blue palette.
3. Or choose three custom colours and click Apply custom theme.
4. The choice is stored on this computer and returns the next time the app opens.

QUICK START ON WINDOWS

1. Extract the complete ZIP folder. Do not run it from inside the ZIP.
2. Double-click START-CHUNESIDE-APP.bat.
3. Keep the black PowerShell window open while using ChuneSide.
4. Microsoft Edge will open the app. If Edge is unavailable, your default browser opens.
5. When finished, close ChuneSide and press Ctrl+C in the black window.

ADD YOUR MUSIC OR VIDEOS

1. Open Test Studio.
2. Add the artist to the Artist database if this is their first release.
3. Click Add release and select that artist from the dropdown.
4. Enter the title, genre, origin and creation type.
5. Choose your MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4 or WebM file.
6. Add cover artwork if you have it, then save.

CHANGE THE TOP HERO IMAGE OR VIDEO

1. Open Test Studio.
2. Find Change hero media.
3. Choose an image or video from your computer.
4. Click Use as hero. A video will play silently and loop.
5. Use Restore original whenever you want the supplied ChuneSide video back.

TEST UNIQUE LIKES AND RANKINGS

- The included “Nigel · Test member” profile can Like each release once.
- Click + Member to create two or three more test listeners.
- Switch members using “Testing as” at the top.
- Each member can add or remove only one Like per release.
- Open Charts to compare This month and All time.
- Choose Guest to confirm that guests can listen but cannot Like or Follow.

INSTALL AS AN EDGE APP (OPTIONAL)

1. Start ChuneSide with the BAT file.
2. In Edge, open the three-dot menu.
3. Choose Apps > Install ChuneSide Offline Test Studio.
4. You can then pin it to Start or the taskbar.
5. Start the BAT file before opening the installed app so its local address is available.

IMPORTANT

- Your songs, videos, artwork, Likes and members stay in this browser on this computer.
- Clearing Edge site data for localhost:8765 will erase the test catalogue.
- Use short or compressed videos first. Very large files may exceed browser storage.
- This offline edition is for private testing. It does not upload anything to the live website.
- The real public edition will use secure member accounts and an online database.
